Polls

A poll is made of a title, a description and one or more queries. Queries can be multiple choices lists, single choice lists, options boxes, text fields or text areas. Choices are added from the "Queries" tab of the poll.

Each poll has its own public page. Polls can also be linked to entries or pages from their edition sidebar, displayed with widgets, or inserted in templates with {{tpl:PollForm}} and {{tpl:PollsOfEntry}}.

Votes

Visitors are identified by a cookie, by their IP address or by both (see plugin configuration). IP addresses are not stored, only a hash of them. Votes can be opened or closed at any time. Text responses are only displayed on public side once they are checked on the results page.
